# Stopping Rule — Committed Before Batch 2, While Nothing Is At Stake

**Written:** 2026-07-25, alongside `research/CONDITIONAL_SEARCH_CHARTER.md`.
Per this project's own repeated practice (H-001's bounded claim, the
FDR ledger built at 6/6 kills, the interim finding before H-005): the
right time to decide a rule is before it matters, not in month 9
staring at a twelfth kill.

**This document is hash-pinned upon commit**, per the charter's
Section 7. Any change to the stopping rule itself must be registered
and justified in the ledger before it takes effect.

---

## The rule

**The Conditional Search (Batch 2, 8-12 adjudications) has exactly two
pre-committed outcomes. There is no third option, and no unregistered
"just a few more ideas" batch.**

### Branch A — A hypothesis survives

If any Batch 2 hypothesis clears every bar in the charter's Section 5
(TRAIN presence, predicted sign pattern matched, dispersion check,
cost+50% stress, >=30 OOS trades per cell):

1. **Single OOS unseal** -- the sealed 2022-2025 data is examined for
   this hypothesis, once, per its own pre-registered OOS test design.
2. **Cost model v2 required before this step matters** -- built in
   parallel during Batch 2 execution (variable spread, swap, weekend
   gap handling), not after a survivor appears. A small real-looking
   effect surviving today's simplistic cost model and dying under
   honest costs is a named risk; the defense is timing, not vigilance.
3. **Pre-register H-Live** -- the live-monitoring kill criteria, in the
   same registry, before any capital or paper-trading exposure.
4. **One quarter of paper trading** as the first live test, with the
   live-vs-backtest distribution comparison as the adjudication -- not
   a formality, an actual pass/fail gate.
5. **Real capital only after all five criteria below are met -- no
   exceptions:**
   - Survived registered TRAIN adjudication and single OOS unseal.
   - Survived cost model v2 + 50% stress.
   - One quarter of paper trading statistically consistent with
     backtest expectation.
   - Pre-registered live kill criteria committed in the ledger.
   - Capital sized so total loss is tuition, not damage.

### Branch B -- Batch 2 completes with zero survivors

If all 8-12 conditional/cross-sectional/event hypotheses kill:

**The Search at 1H/H4 in this five-instrument universe is concluded as
a negative result.** This is written up as a first-class findings
report -- equal rank with any acceptance would have been, not a lesser
deliverable. The report states plainly: no unconditional, no
conditional, no cross-sectional, no (tested) event-anchored structure
survived, across N total hypotheses, M mechanism families, at this
frequency band, in this instrument universe, under retail-representative
costs.

**Two pre-specified sub-branches, decided NOW, not in the moment:**

**B1 -- Regime change (the pre-specified default): the daily-frequency
program.** Rationale, stated now while it's easy to reason about
without result-pressure: at daily frequency, the cost disadvantage that
constrains a retail account shrinks (fewer round-trips per unit of
edge), and capacity constraints that deter institutional capital from a
given inefficiency become less relevant at retail scale -- while the
discipline advantage (ledger, protocol, null-model machinery, FDR
accounting) carries over intact. This is the asymmetry: retail cost
structure matters most at high frequency, and this project's
comparative advantage (discipline, not speed or size) is
frequency-agnostic. The daily-frequency program's own M1/M2 (null
regeneration, ledger wiring) is a PORT of existing infrastructure, not
new engineering -- this is where two years of platform investment pays
its dividend. Registered as a new search program with its own charter
and its own budget, not folded silently into "Batch 3."

**B2 -- Conclusion of the trading objective.** If B1 is also
exhausted, or is deliberately declined, the methodology and the
negative results ARE the project's final output of record. This is
stated as a legitimate, successful outcome -- not a failure to soften.
Per the two-year vision review: "if the honest conclusion is 'no
accessible edge in any searched regime,' writing that down and stopping
is a successful research program. The failure would be drifting away
without writing it down."

## What this rule explicitly forbids

An unregistered Batch 3 of "just a few more ideas" after Batch 2 kills
out. That path is unfalsifiable and infinite -- it is the most likely
failure mode precisely because it never feels like a decision, just one
more reasonable-seeming test. If Batch 2 completes with zero survivors,
the ONLY next actions permitted are B1 (registered daily-frequency
program, its own charter) or B2 (conclusion). Reopening 1H/H4 marginal
or conditional testing in this same universe after Branch B triggers
requires rewriting THIS document, with justification, before doing so
-- not a quiet resumption.

## Why this is decided now, unemotionally

A 0/7 tradable hit rate through Batch 1 is real information: this
project's own hypothesis-generation process has been sampled seven
times, and its measured hit rate on the highest-prior, cheapest
candidates is zero. That's not an indictment -- those were the correct
candidates to clear first -- but it means the null path is the modal
outcome for Batch 2 too, not a hedge clause buried under an optimistic
default narrative. Every roadmap document so far, including the
two-year vision, has detailed the survivor path and given the null path
a paragraph. This document exists specifically to correct that
asymmetry: the null branch (B) is specified in as much operational
detail as the survivor branch (A), because it is, honestly, the more
probable one.
